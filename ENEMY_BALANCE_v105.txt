MOB QUEST v105 - ENEMY BALANCE / RESISTANCE / SKILLS

DESIGN TARGET
- Lv80 + strongest endgame equipment can clear Demon Castle II.
- Enemy level may exceed the stage recommended level; gear, figures, party roles and elemental strategy are part of the intended advantage.

ENEMY STAT PROFILES
- NORMAL: HP 110 + Lv*14 + Lv^2*0.11; ATK/MAG 16 + Lv*2.40 + Lv^2*0.0015; DEF/RES 14 + Lv*2.00 + Lv^2*0.0015
- ELITE: HP 300 + Lv*35 + Lv^2*0.50; ATK/MAG 24 + Lv*2.80 + Lv^2*0.002; DEF/RES 22 + Lv*2.45 + Lv^2*0.002
- BOSS: HP 800 + Lv*60 + Lv^2*1.05; ATK/MAG 34 + Lv*3.40 + Lv^2*0.0025; DEF/RES 28 + Lv*2.80 + Lv^2*0.0025

ELEMENT RESISTANCE
- Normal: own attribute +15%, weakness -10%.
- Elite: own attribute +22%, weakness -8%.
- Boss: own attribute +28%, weakness -5%.
- Existing strong/weak elemental relation remains active in addition to these values.

STATUS RESISTANCE
- Normal base: 15%. Elite base: roughly 34-40%.
- Boss: poison 65%, burn 70%, sleep 80%, flinch 85%, confuse 75%, PARALYZE 100% IMMUNE.
- Boss status duration: all non-paralysis ailments are capped to 1-2 turns; flinch remains max 1 turn.
- Attribute families add natural resistance (fire->burn, thunder->paralysis, earth->flinch, dark->poison etc.).

SKILLS
- Existing authored boss/midboss skills are preserved.
- Enemies without authored skills receive attribute-based formal single-target magic or physical techniques.
- Skill tier rises with enemy level: low / middle / high; third tier from Lv46.
- Higher-level elites can additionally use a status technique fitting their attribute.
- Old generic skill names containing (仮) are no longer used for formalized enemy templates.
