MULTI-ACTION

- 2 actions: offense x0.82
- 3 actions: offense x0.68
- 4 actions: offense x0.58
- Large skill (power >=1.60): reduction is partially relaxed.
- Extreme skill (power >=2.20): nearly full skill identity is retained.
