MOB QUEST v143 REGRESSION REPORT

ROOT CAUSE
- storySayLine() shrank bubbles using a rough character-count formula.
- A 12-character Japanese line could be assigned about 200px. On iOS/Safari the final character could wrap by itself.
- CSS also still used overflow-wrap:anywhere in the last active rule.

FIX
- Story bubble width now uses measured rendered text width plus safe padding.
- Japanese lines of 10+ characters get a 250px minimum, capped by the phone viewport.
- overflow-wrap:anywhere removed by final override; line-break:strict + balanced wrapping used.
- Long unbroken dialogue is split only at punctuation, particles, and semantic clause hints.
- 1-3 character orphan remainders are explicitly prevented.
- Sea Knight Corps lines were manually authored into natural two-line phrases.
- Same semantic paging helper is used by story dialogue, battle dialogue, and castle reports.

PRESERVED
- v142 enemy passives/ultimates and armor selling.
- All v141-v129 patch markers and prior systems.
